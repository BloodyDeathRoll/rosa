Rosa - free surf forecast

Should I surf, when, where? Rosa is a free, fast surf forecast, worldwide: wave height, swell, period, wind, tide and water temperature for every hour, 16 days out, updated four times a day from the NOAA GFS-Wave and ECMWF wave models. The rating formula is public - see how it works and reading the forecast.
