# Rosa — algorithm spec

Read this before touching `pipeline/src/rosa_pipeline/` (transform, score, physics, spectral, extract).
Implement exactly; unit-test all. Why each constant holds its value: `docs/CALIBRATION.md`.

**Wind classification.** θ = smallest angle between wind *source* direction and `offshore_direction`. θ ≤ 45° → Offshore; 45–135° → Cross-shore; > 135° → Onshore.

**Swell energy in window.** Per partition with direction inside `swell_window`: E += H² × T. Partitions outside contribute 0 to score but still display.

**Rating v1 (0–100).** size 0–40 (ramp of transformed height vs spot `ideal.size_m`, peak inside range, taper above) + period 0–20 (linear 6 s → 0 pts, 14 s → 20 pts, dominant in-window partition) + wind 0–40 (offshore ≤ 15 kph = 40; scales down with speed and angle; onshore ≥ 25 kph = 0). **Wind gate:** wind points scale by `min(1, (size_pts + period_pts)/60)` — offshore wind polishes waves that exist, it never creates rating alone. Day-strip `rating_max` uses daylight hours only (dawn–dusk via astral). Buckets: 0-9 FLAT, 10-29 POOR, 30-49 FAIR, 50-69 GOOD, 70+ FIRING.

**Breaker height — hybrid physics, random-sea since 2026-08-28.** Per in-window partition, march the spot's committed depth profile (`data/spots/derived/bathy/{id}.json`) as a RANDOM sea (`physics.surf_height_over_profile`; the input is `(dist_m, depth_m)` pairs from `wet_samples_deep_to_shallow()` — friction needs distance): dispersion → shoaling `Ks` → Snell refraction `Kr`, with **JONSWAP-form bottom friction** (effective Cb ≈ 0.37 m²s⁻³ - the code's 0.038 is applied over g, not g², so it is 10× SWAN's swell value; tuned by eye, see CALIBRATION.md 2026-09-07 - ~25 m substeps between measured samples) and **Battjes–Janssen (1978) breaking dissipation** in place of the old hard γ·d cap (tide still added to depths). **The profile runs out before the sea saturates in ~97–100% of published hours** — close that last stretch with the Komar & Gaughan (1972) breaker index `Hb = 0.39 g^0.2 (T H0'^2)^0.4` fed the **friction-attenuated** offshore height (H0 de-shoaled via `deep_water_equivalent`, refraction iterated to breaking depth) **× 0.79** — K&G(Hs) tracks the top-tenth breaker H1/10 (Caldwell & Aucan 2007, JCR 23(5):1237); ×0.79 (their eq. 4, Rayleigh) publishes the significant breaker instead of the top-tenth wave. **The two answers reconcile as `max(march_surf, 0.79·Hb_KG)` — never as a branch** (2026-08-03; see below). The monochromatic error was regime-dependent: measured 2× hot at poleg (short-period sea over a 12 km shelf) vs Surfline on near-identical inputs, ~0.79 flat on a steep groundswell coast (`docs/CALIBRATION.md` 2026-08-28). Then multiply by `period_rideability` — power-based, **capped at 1.0, attenuates only** (naive physics over-calls short-period Med windswell ~3×). Then combine partitions **by wave field — never RMS** (see below). Then multiply by `wind_face_factor` — **ungraded, see below**.

- **Field combine.** `field_coherence(Δdir, spread_a, spread_b)` = `cos²` taper, 1.0 at zero separation → 0 once Δdir reaches the sum of the two partitions' own spreads. Coherent partitions add their energy in `Hb^2.5` (`FIELD_ENERGY_EXP`, the exact inverse of K&G's 0.4 exponent, so the sum *is* "add offshore energy, apply the index once"); partitions past that arc taper toward the dominant one alone. **`sqrt(Σ Hb²)` was wrong twice over** — it ran the sublinear index (`Hb ~ H0^0.8`) once per partition, so N small partitions beat the one sea they are, and it merged trains crossing at 55° into a single bigger wave. Cost Santa Teresa 1.0–1.8 m vs Surfline 0.6–1.1 (2026-08-03). A single in-window partition is bit-identical to the old path, so the Israel eye frames are untouched. The taper must stay **smooth**: a hard threshold jitters the published height 0.18 m on a 3° direction wander.
- **March/index handoff: take the deeper break, don't branch.** Choosing the march wherever it happened to reach `γ·d` made height rise as the angle worsened (1.170 → 1.303 m across 13°→14° of incidence) and let the *sampling interval* set the answer — the march can only break AT a sampled depth, so a profile stepping 4 m → 1 m clamped a 2 m 14 s swell to `γ·1` = 0.78 m against 2.98 m from solving `H(d) = γd` continuously. 51 of 199 profiles reach 1–2 m, so that fired on exactly their biggest days. K&G sits above linear theory in nearly every real sea state (linear shoaling under-calls the growth just before breaking), so the max is the index almost always; the depth limit remains a real floor for steep short-period seas over a shelf.
- `period_rideability` keys on the partition's **PEAK** period (`rideability_period()`; mean only as a no-Tp fallback). `RIDEABILITY_EXP = 2.2`.
- **`wind_breaker_factor` — height absorbs wind** (open item 4, product call 2026-08-03). Applied to the *combined* breaker height, so display and `size_score` stay one number. Keyed on `U_n/√(g·h_b)` — the shore-normal wind component (`transform.shore_normal_wind_ms`, **positive offshore**) over the wave speed at the breaking depth — because that is the dimensionless number Douglass (1990) parameterised on; a raw-kph multiplier is the wrong form. **Signed:** onshore wind moves the break point offshore and lowers the breaker; offshore wind breaks it closer in and *larger*. Clamped to ±50%. **`WIND_BREAKER_COEFF = 0.0` — the term is inert and must stay inert until a frame graded in a >30 kph onshore blow can set it.** Nothing else can: the buoys show Open-Meteo's offshore Hs is not wind-biased, and all five calibration frames sit at 1.9–23 kph where the term is ~1.0. See `docs/CALIBRATION.md` 2026-08-03.
- Because rideability saturates above ~10 s, the exponent is in practice an **Israel/Portugal knob**; the breaker-index closure is a **long-period-region correction**. They separate cleanly.
- **The ray is ordered by DISTANCE, not depth - the march skips any sample deeper than its reference** (2026-08-15). `wet_samples_deep_to_shallow()` reverses the profile by distance from the spot, so its name describes the usual case, not a guarantee: **87 of 199 committed rays dip deeper somewhere inside the 18 km point** (GEBCO shelves, banks, canyons) and **17 have their deepest wet sample there**, meaning `depths[0]` is not the deep-water reference for those. Shoaling theory has nothing to say about a wave moving back into deeper water, and marching one anyway is not merely inaccurate - Snell returns `sin_a` just under 1 and `Kr = sqrt(cos a_ref / cos alpha)` is **unbounded** as `alpha → 90°`. Measured before the fix: 24.96 m in 32 m of water from a 3.0 m swell (cantinho-bah-a), 192.7 m from a 4.0 m swell (zvulun), returned as *breaker heights* and published. Such samples are now skipped exactly as the grazing case is; the shallower samples still decide the break. **Fleet-wide bit-for-bit no-op** (199-spot payload SHA-256 identical), and the honest bound is worth keeping: on realistic inputs it fired **0 times in 13.68 M cells**, because the blow-up band is ≤1e-5 s wide in period against Open-Meteo's ~0.01 s granularity. Latent and unbounded, never an observed incident. **The reference itself is still the farthest sample, not the deepest** - `d_ref = max(...)` is one line and would make `Kr <= 1` structurally, but it moves published numbers (13/199 spots, 55/76,416 ratings, max |Δ| 0.014 m), so it is gated on a Stage-0 A/B: OPEN-ITEMS item 15.
- **Nothing downstream sanity-checks breaker MAGNITUDE - the check lives at precompute time or nowhere.** `run` warns (never clamps) when `h_break_mid` exceeds **4×** the largest partition reaching the spot. No payload key, no constant that scores, so it cannot move a rating. **Derive that ratio from a LIVE fleet run, never from the committed capture** - the capture is one Mediterranean point (short-period windswell, max ratio 2.012, nothing over 3), and a threshold of 3 set from it fired on 19 live hours, every one a long-period Pacific groundswell on Central America at Tp 14.9-17.9 s. A 0.40 m 17.5 s swell shoaling to 1.30 m is what a groundswell does, not a defect. Live: 16,961 published hours with a usable sea, p99.9 = 2.95, true max = 3.25, so 4× clears real physics with headroom and still catches the class this exists for (the refraction singularity produced 8×-57×). **"Reaching the spot" means `spread_exposure(p, window) > 0`, NOT the hard `in_window()` bearing test** - the march weights every partition by `sqrt(spread_exposure)`, so a train whose fan merely clips the window still feeds the breaker; the hard test undercounts the sea and fires on 42 sound hours.
- **Never append invented shallow samples** to a profile — the answer becomes γ × whatever depth was made up.
- Display as range h±20%, snapped to 0.1 m, widened to a real ≥0.1 m spread ("0.1–0.1" isn't a range).
- Tune γ / rideability exponent / residual coeff from `docs/CALIBRATION.md` + the Stage-0 scorecard only. **`height_coeff` is 1.0 at all 199 spots.**
- No-profile fallback only: `q(T) = clamp((T/10)^1.4, 0.3, 1.2)` (`PERIOD_EXP`).

**Spread-aware exposure.** A partition is not one bearing — its energy fans over ±20–35° (`spectral.parametric_spread_deg`, CDIP-tuned; `SPREAD_LONG_SWELL_DEG` 24). `spread_exposure()` integrates the soft window (flat 1.0 inside `swell_window`, cosine taper to 0 over a 25° shoulder, `EXPOSURE_SHOULDER_DEG`) over that fan and returns the ENERGY fraction reaching the spot (amplitude = sqrt). The incidence fed to refraction is `arriving_direction()` — the window edge the energy comes in over, not the fan's mean — capped at `GRAZING_INCIDENCE_DEG` (85°) so windows reaching past the shore normal are honoured, not cancelled. Energy/kJ uses the same exposure as the height, so a row can't show surf beside 0 kJ.

> **Invariant:** a metre offshore can never read ~0 at the spot *when it arrives from a direction the coastline can see*. Any zero-audit must also require an in-window partition, or it re-flags lee shores forever.

**Peak period source.** `best_match` resolves to `meteofrance_wave`, which serves **no** Tp. `gwam` (DWD global WAM) rides the same marine request as a **Tp-only** source (`fetch.TP_MODEL`, ~7.3-day coverage) — it must never become the horizon tail. Coverage ~25–40% per region; secondary/tertiary partitions have none (the API has no such variable).

**Display ≠ scoring — deliberate, do not "fix" by unifying.** All swell readouts show the **PEAK** period `peak_period ?? period` (surf-report convention, and what the kJ column derives from). `h_break`'s rideability factor also keys on peak. Still on the **MEAN** period: the dispersion/shoaling/refraction march inside `surf_height_over_profile`, and `period_score` (the rating's Period component + the web's Period bar). Disclosed to users at `#/about/reading` — **keep it in sync**.

**Human phrase** (h_break midpoint): <0.3 shin-high · 0.3–0.5 knee · 0.5–0.8 waist · 0.8–1.2 chest · 1.2–1.5 head-high · 1.5–2 overhead · 2–3 well overhead · >3 double-overhead+.
**Wetsuit** (from SST): >24° rashguard · 19–24 spring/2 mm · 17–19 3/2 · 14–17 4/3 · 12–14 5/4+boots · 9–12 5/4 hooded+gloves · <9 6/5 hooded.

**Swell-event detection.** A contiguous ≥12 h window where in-window energy ≥ 2× trailing 48 h median and rating ≥ GOOD at peak. Emit `{start, peak, end, peak_height, direction}`.

**Tides.** Open-Meteo Marine `sea_level_height_msl` (MeteoFrance SMOC, ~8 km hourly) — **total** water level (astronomical + surge). Feeds the breaking depth (`d_break = profile_depth + tide`) and publishes as a per-hour series. The tide shifts the whole committed profile before the march (`physics.tide_shifted_samples`, since 2026-09-08): a sample that DRIES at this level is the shoreline - replaced by the point where the shifted depth crosses zero, interpolated between it and the last wet sample - and everything shoreward of it is dropped. It is never deleted as a hole: deleting it handed the march a profile ending a whole GEBCO segment further out, and the published height stepped from 1.29 m to 0.00 m across 5 cm of tide (`docs/CALIBRATION.md` 2026-09-08). Med spots micro-tidal (~0.15 m, sparkline only); macro-tidal spots (Costa Rica ~1.5–2 m) genuinely move the forecast.

**Sun.** `astral`; show first light / sunrise / sunset / last light.

**`closure`** (`transform.partition_closure`, per-hour payload key) = partition RMS ÷ Open-Meteo's own total `wave_height`. **Diagnostic only — nothing scores on it.** A low ratio says energy is missing but not its direction or period, and inventing those is against the premise. **Do NOT scale partitions to close it** — a confidence penalty is the honest option. Fleet by region: israel 1.00 · japan 0.97 · philippines 0.96 · nicaragua/hawaii 0.95 · portugal/costa-rica 0.94 · california 0.91 · sri-lanka 0.90.

**`agreement_score`** (`confidence.agreement`) = `1 - CoV/COV_MAX` over the member Hs, spread as `pstdev` (÷n). That estimator is biased low by a k-dependent factor (E[pstdev] ≈ 0.56σ at k=2 vs 0.84σ at k=5), so the same physical disagreement scores higher with fewer members - and the blend arm (default since 2026-08-26) runs at k=2 where the OM arm ran 4-5, with k also falling per hour as `gwam` and regional members drop out. **Do not pool `agreement_score` distributions across the 2026-08-26 boundary or across `n_models`** (both are published per hour). A k-aware spread (unbiased `stdev` or a per-k table from the archived pairs) is a CALIBRATION.md entry first, then wiring (pb_0049 - it moves published confidence fleet-wide). OPEN-ITEMS 29.
